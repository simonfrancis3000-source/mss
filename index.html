01Articulated G1 with hands

The scene uses the Menagerie G1 hand model, including its head, torso, arms, hands, legs and feet.

02Stable demonstration pose

The pelvis is constrained for this tabletop task. This prototype does not demonstrate balance, walking or real robot capability.

03Physics-driven grasp limits

The cup remains a free MuJoCo body; finger contact and friction must hold it. The three-finger hand has low-torque position actuators and no fingertip force sensors, so this does not establish general grasping ability.
