AMÏA / BEHAVIOURAL INTELLIGENCE Understands how you want your companion to behave.

AMÏA explores your preferences, boundaries, social style and decision-making through natural conversation. It asks one focused question at a time and captures only what you state or confirm.

PEXI / CAPABILITY VALIDATION Checks what a particular robot can actually do.

PEXI maps the behavioural intention to a target robot's declared capabilities and safety requirements. A preference is not a promise that a robot can physically perform the action.

Your companion should be considerate and capable in society, not simply obedient. Training is progressive: there is no need to complete every scenario in one sitting.
